Business model

Asset-light fashion commerce

Retailers and brands retain inventory ownership. Riggy's proposed role is discovery, AI styling, product matching, checkout and fulfilment coordination.

1. Seller inventorySKU, size, colour, price and location availability are synced into the marketplace.
2. AI discoveryAI grounds recommendations in catalogue and inventory data rather than inventing products.
3. Look fulfilmentPrefer a single nearby node; if an item is unavailable, customer-approved substitution is offered.
4. MonetisationIllustrative model: seller commission + customer delivery/convenience fee; advertising can be added after scale.
Prototype only: retailer participation, live inventory, commission rates and delivery SLAs are not confirmed partnerships or production claims.
